Global Angler is an offshore fishing game and live weather app. Pick a
fishing spot, cast a line, and reel in fish — with the real conditions for
the spot you are fishing.

Live data for every spot:
- Forecast from Open-Meteo: temperature, wind, and weather
- Swell and waves from the Open-Meteo Marine API
- Real tides from NOAA for the Florida Keys (Key West)

If live data is unavailable, the app says so and shows an offline estimate
instead of pretending the data is live.

The game: casting with a power meter, bite windows, streaks, boats and a
fleet to upgrade, an aquarium to fill, tournaments, quests, and achievements
across 19 locations around the world.

Global Angler is free and open source. It has no accounts, no ads, and no
analytics. Your save stays on your phone.
