Privacy Policy for Global Angler

Effective date: November 12, 2026
Developer: Elijah Wougamon
App: Global Angler (package com.livewire.globalangler)

Data we collect

None. Global Angler does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and it does not know who you are. It does not access your device's live location.

What stays on your phone

Your game progress, settings, and saved data are stored only on your device (in the app's local storage). Nothing is uploaded to any server by the developer.

Weather lookups

To show live weather, the app asks public weather services for the forecast at the fishing spot you pick:

  • Open-Meteo (forecast and marine swell/waves) — the app sends the fishing spot's coordinates and receives the weather for that spot.
  • NOAA CO-OPS (tides for Florida Keys) — the app sends the tide station number and receives the tide times.

The coordinates sent are the preset coordinates of the fishing spots built into the game, not your location. No personal information is sent to these services. They are public weather services; their own privacy policies apply to what they receive.

Third-party libraries

The app loads a few open libraries from public content networks when it starts: Tailwind CSS, Lucide icons, Google Fonts, and the canvas-confetti library. Those networks see a normal web request for the file (including your IP address, as with any web request). The app sends them no personal information.

Donations

The Donate button in the app shows a PayPal QR code. Scanning it opens PayPal, where any donation is handled entirely by PayPal under PayPal's own privacy policy. The app itself sends nothing to PayPal.

Children

Global Angler is a general-audience fishing game. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer, Elijah Wougamon, through the app's Google Play listing.
